    E-WASTE
    THE JOURNEY

    Walk through a 3D version of the E-Waste Management giga map. Follow a discarded phone from a house in India to the informal sector, and find the way to break the loop.

    Problem statementPeople still do not know what to do with their e-waste once its useful life ends, and the real mismanagement starts when it flows into the informal sector.

    How it works

    You are a collector walking the giga map. Your old phone, Ping, floats along with you. Each district finishes when its tasks are ticked and the gate opens.

    • Scroll to walk (mouse wheel, trackpad, W / S, the up / down arrows, or drag up on a phone). The collector follows one track and stops at every point: read the info card, watch the item come aboard, then scroll again to go on.
    • Side-step with A / D or the left / right arrows (or drag sideways) to dodge toxic clouds.
    • Everything happens by itself when you arrive. Decisions (customs, the workbench) open a card to answer. Scroll back any time to read a stop again.
    • Green clouds are toxic fumes in the informal sector. Walk round them.
    • Gates close the track until a district's tasks are ticked. Esc leaves the game. M mutes sound.
