Components

  • 16 pieces: 4 of each colour.
    3 stars walk as per track direction. 1 reverse star walks anticlockwise and clockwise, irrespective of track direction, in the first circle.
  • Play board.
  • Dice of 6 numbers.

Setup

Each player chooses one of the 4 colours (green, yellow, red or blue) and places the 4 pieces of that colour near the corresponding starting circle. A single die is thrown to determine movement. A player must throw a 6 or 1 to move a piece from the starting circle onto the first circle on the track. All the block coloured steps in the layout are safe zones.

Rules

Players take turns in a clockwise order; highest throw of the die starts.

Each throw, the player decides which star to move. A star simply moves along the track direction (the arrows on the board) around the first track by the number thrown. If no star can legally move according to the number thrown, play passes to the next player.

After completing its first track, the star enters the second track from the bridge of its own colour. A star moves around the second track in the same way, by the number thrown.

A throw of 6 gives another turn.

If a piece lands on a piece of a different colour, the piece jumped upon is returned to its starting circle.

If a piece lands on a pipeline then it directly moves to the inner circle. It's a one way movement.

Winning

When a piece has circumnavigated the board, it proceeds up the Home circle with pearls. A star can only be moved onto the home triangle by an exact throw.

The first player to move all 4 pieces into the home triangle wins.

Boards for 2, 3 and 4 players

The board can be played with 2, 3 and 4 players as the outer and inner circle steps are divisible by 2, 3 and 4. Attachments are placed on top of the main board as per the playing members.

How this version counts

The outer circle has 48 steps and the inner circle 24. A piece enters by its own colour block, walks the whole outer circle, steps over its coloured bridge, walks the whole inner circle and climbs its colour lane to the pearl. The coloured bridge and lane blocks count as steps. A reverse star may go either way round on the outer circle and takes its bridge after a full lap either way. The four pipeline steps on the outer circle lead to the inner circle.
