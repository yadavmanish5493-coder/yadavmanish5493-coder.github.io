Learn Tables and become the Master of Mathematics

A board game that makes memorising multiplication tables a game, not a scolding.

HOW TO PLAY TABLE TOP

1Roll two dice (2 to 12) and hop that many tiles. Every tile is a number from 1 to 29.
2On a number tile, answer that table × your dice total. Right answer: +10 coins. Wrong: nothing. A free tile can then be bought.
3Land on someone's table and you pay the entry fee first. Answer wrong and you pay the table price too.
4Stuck? Pull the number slider to check the table. You start with 50 coins. Most coins after 15 rounds wins.
